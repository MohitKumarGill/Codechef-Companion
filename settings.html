CodeChef Companion — Settings

ChatGPT integration

By default, 🤖 Ask ChatGPT opens ChatGPT in a new tab with the problem already pasted into the prompt. You press Enter there, copy the code, and paste it back with 📋. This works with your free ChatGPT account — no key required.

⚠️ Your key is stored in this browser's extension storage only. It is sent to api.openai.com and nowhere else. Each problem costs roughly $0.001–$0.01 depending on the model and problem length. You can revoke the key at platform.openai.com/api-keys.

Toolbar position

If the CodeChef toolbar ended up somewhere you can't reach, reset it here.

Data

All your saved problems are stored locally in this browser.
